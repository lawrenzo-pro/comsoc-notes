# Optical Transmission
#### The road behind every digital Experience

## The Fibre Optic Cable
  - Core:
  - Cladding
  - Primary Coating
  - Buffer
  - Water Blocking Layer
  - Dielectric Strength Members
  - Outer Jacket
  ### Types
    - Single Mode: One wavelength
    - Multi Mode: Multiple wavelengths
  ### Limits
    - Attenuation: Signal decay
    - Chromatic Dispersion(CD) - different wavelengths travel at different speeds
    - Polarization Mode Dispersion
    - Non-Linear effect :
    - Connector and splice losses
    - Reflection
    - Bending Loss
