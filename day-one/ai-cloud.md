## The Hyperscaler

- Defn of Cloud: On demand delivery of IT services and resources over the internet with as pay-as-you-go pricing
- renting computing the way you buy electricity from the grid.

## What makes a provider a hyperscaler
1. Global Scale
2. Custom silicon
3. Private backbone
4. Hundreds of services

## How a hyperscaler is built
- Region - area with more than 3+ isolated availability zones.
- Availability zone - one or more data centres with independent power.Run across AZs and one failure won't take it down.
- Edge locations - hundreds of smaller sites, including Africa that cache content close to users(Amazon cloudfront)

## Inside The AI cloud

### AI, machine learning and generative AI

- AI: Performs tasks that normally need human intelligence
- Machine Learning - learn patterns from data instead of following fixed rules
- Deep Learning - ML using multilayered NNs
- Generative AI - creates new content such as text, image and code powered by foundation models.
- Agentic AI - plans and takes actions across tools and systems to reach a goal
