# Designing a Resilient, Affordable and Sustainable Community Network

## What are community networks?
- Telecommunications or internet infrastructure built,owned, managed and operated by a local community to provide affordable voice or internet connectivity and digital services

### Traits
- Community Participation
- Local Ownership
- Relevant services
- Affordable connectivity
- Digital Inclusion
- Relevant Services
- Participant, not customer

### Examples
- Tanda Community Network(Kibera) : Urban Marginalised communities in kibera slum focused on local connectivity solutions.
- Namelok wireless : Serving indigineous communities in Kajiado region such as Maasais

### Technology
- Hybrid Network infrastructure (Wireless and Wired) managed by a community led governance model.
### Key Challenge
- Digital Divide

## Wireless Technology
### Wireless Technologies overview
- Many start with wireless technologies for various reasons
1. Affordability - less complex for the first time
2. Unlicenced spectrum - using the ISM Band
3. Challenging Environments - Some areas may not be ideal for fibre optic
4. Scalability - easier to scale and grow
### Types of Wireless Networks
- Point to Point - Direct connection between two locations.
- Point to Multipoint - One central that connects multiple endpoints directly
- Mesh Network - Nodes connect dynamically, expanding coverage and resilience. If one path fails, data automatically reroutrs through other nodes.
### Core Components
#### Hardware
- Routers direct data between networks and devices
- Access points provide wireless
- Antennas extend and focus wireless signals
