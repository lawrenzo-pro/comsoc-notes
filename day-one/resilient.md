# Designing a Resilient, Affordable and Sustainable Community Network

## What are community networks
- Telecommunications or internet infrastructure built,owned, managed and operated by a local community to provide affordable voice or internet connectivity and digital services

### Traits
- Community Participation
- Local Ownership
- Relevant services
- Affordable connectivity
- Digital Inclusion
- Relevant Services
- Participant, not customer

### Examples
- Tanda Community Network(Kibera) : Urban Marginalised communities in kibera slum focused on local connectivity solutions.
- Namelok wireless : Serving indigineous communities in Kajiado region such as Maasais

### Technology
- Hybrid Network infrastructure (Wireless and Wired) managed by a community led governance model.
### Key Challenge
- Digital Divide

## Pillars of Community Networks
### 1. Policy and Governance 
  #### Outside Governance:
  - Infrastructure
  - Spectrum License
  -Services
  #### Inside Governance
  - Team/ Organisation
  - Structure
### 2. Community : People you provide services for
  - Schools
  - Gvt Office
  - Business
  #### Opinion Leaders: Chiefs, Pastors
  
### 3. Resources
  #### Money is not the main goal: but you need it
  #### Human Resources
   - Expert Volunteers
   - Finance Persons
   - Tech Team(Skills)
   ### Funding Resources
### 4. Infrastructure
  #### Network and Infrastructure must be in mind
   - Uplink (Backhaul Provider)
   - Wayleaves eg. Fiber
   - Towers
### 5. Services
  #### What value can your network bring?
  - Your customers ie Farmers, Businesses (How can your network help them tap in the global market)
  - Local services offline, content etc
