# Telecommunications Regulation, Licensing and Spectrum Management In Kenya

## Authorization Mechanism
### Service Licence and Infrastructure Scope
- What you want to provide, ie laying fibre, using cell towers or delivering over the top services

### Resource Assignment
- Radio Spectrum, Numbering Blocks, IP routes and statutory grants
-
### Internatonal vs domestic grants
- ITU international frequency allocation or vendor specification.

## Standards & Numbering and Type approval

1. Equipment Type (Compatibility, safety)
2. Standards Development (Certification of equipment, Eco sustainability)
3. Management of numbering resources (Fair and Transparent Allocation, Planning and Coordination)
4. Trade Facilitation (National Single Window System, Import Permit Clearance)

## Spectrum Management

- To ensure efficient utilization of frequency spectrum resource, the Authority has set strategic objectives on spectrum management
- Administrative and Technical procedures to work with spectrum management systems to facilitate:
      Planning -> Licensing -> Monitoring -> Compliance
