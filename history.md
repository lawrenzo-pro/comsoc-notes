## Before The Web, There was Email

### The Internet's First Job

### Kenya's early Connection
- Around 1992, public email and limited browsing through ARCC
- October 1995, first online link, a 32kbps leased to the UK
- The state monopoly called internet services illegal resale of leased lines

``` A monopoly at the border decides what a whole country can do online ```

## The Kenya Internet Exchange (KIXP)
- Late 2000: Kenyan ISPs set up an exchange to keep local traffic local
- About a week later, telcom kenya asked the regulator to close it
- Early 2002:It restarted under a new licence, the first issued to an IXP

``` Engineers and Regulators together decide how fast and how cheap a countries internet can be. ```
